It sounds like a very interesting and practically important application.

Given your setting, especially the need to detect relatively small and previously unseen objects with only a few LiDAR returns, I think your current geometric pipeline is a reasonable starting point.

Since many of the target obstacles are unknown and may be very small, moving-object or temporal-change detection could be particularly relevant. You may want to have a look at the following two works:

1. H. Wu et al., “Moving Event Detection from LiDAR Point Streams,” Nature Communications, 15, 345, 2024.

2. V. Bhandari et al., “Moving Object Segmentation in Point Cloud Data Using Hidden Markov Models,” The International Journal of Robotics Research, 45(8), 1165C1194, 2026.

Both are interesting because they exploit temporal information in LiDAR sequences and do not rely purely on recognizing predefined object categories.

Hope these references are helpful!
